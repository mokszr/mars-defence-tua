Türkiye Uzay Ajansı · Uzay Kuvvetleri Komutanlığı · 2071

    
MarsSavunma

    
The first Turkish colony on Mars is online, and today is the defense drill. The laser in the command center must always fire at the asteroid closest to Mars. Its fire control is a PriorityQueue: every tick each asteroid is removed, moved closer and added back, and queue.peek() names the target.

    

      
The laserFires on its own at the head of the queue. Watch the inspector on the right reorder as asteroids close in.

      
Your missilesClick anywhere in the sky to launch. They reload over time and hit everything in the blast.

      
Between wavesSpend credits on power, extra turrets that take the 2nd and 3rd queue entries, and smarter comparators.
